# Code of conduct

PocketDays is a small, friendly project. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow these rules.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent and critique ideas, not people.
- Keep feedback specific and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and people who use the project in ways you would not.
- Respect privacy. Do not post other people's personal data, expense data, diary exports or private diary links.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without consent.
- Spam, self-promotion unrelated to the project, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block contributors who break these rules. To report a problem, use the contact details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
